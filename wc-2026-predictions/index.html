Scoring Rules

  • 6 pts
    Perfect Knockout Prediction: If someone predicts penalty correctly and also predicts correctly who will win based on that, then they get 6 points!
  • 5 pts
    Perfect Prediction: Correct winner and exact scoreline.
  • 3 pts
    Correct Winner + Semi-Exact Score: Correct winner, and at least one team's goals were predicted correctly (e.g. predicted 2-0, actual 2-1).
  • 2 pts
    Correct Winner only: Correct winner, but both team scores were wrong.
  • 1 pt
    Consolation Score: Incorrect winner, but one of the team's scores was correct (e.g. predicted 2-3, actual 2-1).
  • Var
    Draw Formula: Predicted draw & actual draw (but wrong scoreline). Awarded 4 / D points, where D is the absolute difference in total goals. (e.g. predicted 3-3, actual 0-0 gives 4/6 = 0.67 pts).
  • 0 pts
    Completely Wrong: Incorrect winner and completely incorrect scoreline.
  • Avg Err
    Average Goal Error: Measures how far off your predictions are in terms of goals. For each evaluated match, it takes the absolute difference between your predicted goals and the actual goals for both teams, and averages this over all completed matches. A lower value means your predictions are closer to the real scorelines.
