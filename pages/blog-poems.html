Beyond

When the sun has set, and the sky ablaze with fiery stars
When the earth has cooled, and the zephyr blows at lazy pace

When the mortals have slept, and the darkness whispers silently
When the Endless roam Earth, and people dream his infinite realm

When the demons descend, and the angels flutter wings to fly
When the morality blurs, and the ecstasy of passionate hearts peak

Shrouded in life, and propelled by impetus of immortal love
I shall meet you, beyond the scorching gaze of the universe!

Death of a Universe


In an ever expanding universe
We move closer defying darkness
Violating Hubble's sacred law
As we gravitate towards the center
Amidst the confluence of our
Own twisted, convoluted emotions
Rupture the fabric or space and time
Conceive our own ephemeral continuum
We embrace, we annihilate the other
In a brilliant burst of infinite energy
The dimensions ignite, forces crumble

And the cosmos burns in brilliance
Of a billion ablazed supernovae

Colorless


Far edge of a long night
Sky attuned to lunar theme

Angels string the cloud
A million colors erupt out

Coupled to memories in past
An iota of saudade in present

Dawn draws near, unabated
I beg moon for another ray

Sun's callous rays spill over
Realm turns white, colorless!
Symphonic Consilience

Unbounded passion fills the void of space
A saudade past suffocates in the embrace
Nightmares transmute into delicate dream
Moonless night turns into a lunar theme
Erratic turbulent breaths gently alleviated
Eventuality of myriad of touches, uninhibited
Emotional anarchy oft calmly triumphed
In anon her brilliance had my soul seduced
Routing a reluctant mind's perennial resilience
The two hearts beat in symphonic consilience

मन में निराशा जगह बनाती
भोर हुए, आस दिलाने को
आती क्षितिज की पहली लाली

Dream a dream of us!

Will you buy that book from the used book store down by the river?
The words are awkward and lines cheesy, but that's not why I read them on weekends
Will you go to a game of test cricket on a beautiful Caribbean island?
I didn't understand the rules of game and the strategies, but that's not why I still follow your team
Let's get our portrait drawn together by an artist down at 42nd
The images are twisted and distorted, but that's not why it hangs on the wall by my desk
Let's get coffee at the cosy cafe on the 14th avenue
It's weak and too sweet, but that's not why I still brew them in the same way
Shall we take a ride in Red Line from the Square?
The car was dirty and smelly, but that's not why I still frequent the route every week
Shall we indulge in forever love in the realm of Lord Oneiros?
The relationship was ephemeral and inconsequential, but that's not why I hold on to them so tightly
I dreamt a dream of you!
